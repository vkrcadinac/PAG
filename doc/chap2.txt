  [1X2.1-13 ConjugationFilter[101X

        partition [3Xgl[103X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3XInvariant[103X:="CycleLengths"  The  cycle  lengths of all elements of [10Xg[110X on
        [10X[1..[110X[3Xv[103X[10X][110X are used to partition [3Xgl[103X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3XInvariant[103X:=[10Xfunction[110X  Any  function that is an invariant of permutation
        isomorphism can also be given and is then used to partition [3Xgl[103X.[133X
